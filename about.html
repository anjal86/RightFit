OUR APPROACH

People first.
Always.

Meaningful allied health support begins with listening and recognising each person's unique strengths, aspirations and circumstances.

✳ WHAT RIGHTFIT STANDS FOR

There is no single way to make progress.

At RightFit, the focus is on support that fits around the individual rather than asking the individual to fit a template.

We bring together four allied health disciplines — Physiotherapy, Behaviour Support (BSP), Occupational Therapy and Speech Pathology — with a shared focus on everyday wellbeing and independence.

HOW WE THINK ABOUT CARE

Support that feels human.

01

Respect for the individual

Taking time to understand each person's needs, preferences and goals.

02

Working in partnership

Keeping individuals, families and supporters involved in the conversation where appropriate.

03

Supporting independence

Focusing on abilities, confidence and participation in everyday life.
