OUR APPROACH

People first.
Always.

Meaningful allied health care begins with active listening. We recognise each individual’s unique strengths, life context, and vision for independence.

✳ WHAT RIGHTFIT STANDS FOR

There is no single template for making progress.

At RightFit, our name represents our core principle: support must fit around the individual, rather than forcing the person to adapt to a rigid system.

We unite four complementary disciplines — Physiotherapy, Positive Behaviour Support (BSP), Occupational Therapy, and Speech Pathology. By collaborating under one cohesive framework, we ensure our participants experience seamless, integrated care without fragmented communication.

Support that feels human.

CLINICAL EXCELLENCE

Rigorous standards &
trusted governance.

Our clinical practice adheres strictly to national Australian health codes and disability safeguarding regulations.

AHPRA Registered Clinicians

Our Physiotherapists and Occupational Therapists are registered with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency, adhering to strict professional standards.

Speech Pathology Australia

Our Speech Pathologists hold Certified Practising Speech Pathologist (CPSP) accreditation with Speech Pathology Australia (SPA).

NDIS Safeguards Commission

Our Behaviour Support Practitioners operate under the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission rules, prioritising positive behaviour support and reducing restrictive practices.

Privacy & Data Rights

We strictly protect participant health records and personal information in full compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APP).
