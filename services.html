Physiotherapy

Movement, mobility & everyday confidence

Our AHPRA-registered physiotherapists help participants build functional movement, restore mobility, manage physical conditions, and increase balance and endurance in natural everyday environments.

  • Comprehensive motor skill and mobility assessments
  • Prescription and trials for walking aids and mobility equipment
  • Neurological and physical disability rehabilitation
  • Strength, posture, coordination, and pain management programmes

Behaviour Support (BSP)

Understanding behaviour, promoting positive wellbeing

Operating under NDIS Safeguards Commission guidelines, our practitioners deliver evidence-based Positive Behaviour Support (PBS). We view behaviour as communication and build strategies that uphold dignity and minimise restrictive practices.

  • Functional Behaviour Assessments (FBA) and environmental reviews
  • Interim and Comprehensive Positive Behaviour Support Plans
  • Upskilling families, educators, and support workers on proactive strategies
  • Safe reduction and elimination of restrictive practices

RIGHTFIT SERVICE 03

Occupational Therapy

Building everyday independence & capacity

Occupational Therapy focuses on enabling you to participate in activities that matter most — from self-care and cooking to schooling, work, and community life. We assess capabilities, routines, and physical environments to unlock independence.

  • Activities of Daily Living (ADL) assessments and skill development
  • Sensory profiling and emotional regulation strategies
  • Assistive Technology (AT) recommendations and trials
  • Home modifications and Supported Independent Living (SIL/SDA) reports

RIGHTFIT SERVICE 04

Speech Pathology

Communication, connection & swallowing support

Our certified Speech Pathologists support speech clarity, language comprehension, social communication, and mealtime safety. We believe every person has the fundamental right to communicate and connect with the world around them.

  • Speech sound development and language comprehension therapies
  • Augmentative & Alternative Communication (AAC) systems and devices
  • Mealtime management and Dysphagia (swallowing) assessments
  • Social communication and pragmatic language support

TRANSPARENT PRICING

Funding options &
how to access care.

We work with multiple Australian funding streams to make accessing quality allied health straightforward.

NDIS Funding

Plan & Self-Managed NDIS

We bill directly under standard NDIS Price Limits with zero hidden out-of-pocket charges. We support Capacity Building (CB Daily Living, CB Relationships) and Core funding categories.

Medicare CDM / EPC

GP Management Plans

Participants with a chronic or complex medical condition may be eligible for up to 5 rebated allied health visits per calendar year under a GP Chronic Disease Management plan.

Private & Self-Funded

Health Insurance & Direct

No referral is required for private sessions. You can claim through your private health insurer extras cover, or self-fund appointments based on our transparent fee schedule.
