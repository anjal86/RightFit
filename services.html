Physiotherapy

Movement, mobility & everyday confidence

Physiotherapy generally focuses on movement, physical function and managing challenges that affect everyday activities. Support may involve understanding your goals and exploring ways to improve comfort, strength, movement or confidence.

Behaviour Support (BSP)

Understanding behaviour, supporting wellbeing

Behaviour Support takes a person-centred approach to understanding behaviour and the circumstances surrounding it. The aim is to explore positive, respectful strategies that support quality of life and participation.

Occupational Therapy

More independence in the everyday

Occupational Therapy focuses on the activities people want and need to do in their daily lives. Support can explore practical skills, routines, environments and strategies for greater participation and independence.
