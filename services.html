Physiotherapy

Movement, Mobility & Physical Independence

Our AHPRA-registered physiotherapists work with children, adolescents, and adults across Australia to optimise movement, manage neurological conditions, improve respiratory function, and build physical confidence in natural daily environments.

Key Assessments & Reports

  • Comprehensive Functional Mobility & Gait Analysis
  • Assistive Technology (AT) Prescriptions (Wheelchairs, Walkers)
  • Falls Risk, Balance & Postural Alignment Assessments
  • Detailed NDIS Review & Plan Reassessment Reports

Therapeutic Interventions

  • Neurological Rehabilitation (Cerebral Palsy, Stroke, MS)
  • Aquatic Physiotherapy (Hydrotherapy) & Pool Programs
  • Strength, Conditioning & Motor Milestone Programs
  • Chest Physiotherapy & Respiratory Care Protocols

Positive Behaviour Support (BSP)

Understanding Behaviour, Upholding Dignity & Promoting Wellbeing

Operating strictly under the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, our Behaviour Support Practitioners view all behaviour as communication. We develop respectful, proactive strategies that improve quality of life and reduce or eliminate regulated restrictive practices.

Key Assessments & Lodgements

  • Functional Behaviour Assessments (FBA) & Root-Cause Analysis
  • Interim Behaviour Support Plans (developed & lodged within 1 month)
  • Comprehensive Behaviour Support Plans (lodged within 6 months)
  • Restrictive Practice Elimination & Human Rights Reviews

Evidence-Based Interventions

  • Proactive Environmental Modifications & Calming Spaces
  • Coaching & Upskilling for Support Workers, Carers, and Educators
  • Emotional Regulation, Coping Tools & Sensory Strategies
  • Safe Crisis De-escalation Plans (Non-aversive Techniques)

NDIS Funding

Plan-Managed & Self-Managed

We bill strictly in accordance with current NDIS Price Limits. Therapy is covered under Capacity Building – Improved Daily Living (Category 15) and Improved Relationships (Category 11). Travel is claimed transparently per NDIS MMM guidelines.

Medicare Australia

Chronic Disease Management (CDM/EPC)

Eligible patients referred by an Australian General Practitioner under a GP Management Plan (GPMP / Team Care Arrangement) can receive Medicare rebates for up to 5 allied health therapy visits per calendar year.

SERVICES & CLINICAL INTAKE FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about referrals, assessments, and therapy delivery with RightFit.

What NDIS line items does RightFit bill under? +

For Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, and Speech Pathology, we bill under Capacity Building – Improved Daily Living (line item 15_056_0128_1_3 or 15_048_0128_1_3). For Positive Behaviour Support, we bill under Capacity Building – Improved Relationships (line item 11_022_0110_7_3 Specialist Behavioural Intervention Support and 11_023_0110_7_3 Behaviour Management Plan). We always operate strictly within standard NDIS Price Limits.

How does clinician travel work under the NDIS? +

When our clinicians visit your home, school, or community setting, travel is billed in accordance with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements (up to 30 minutes in metropolitan areas MMM1–3, or up to 60 minutes in regional areas MMM4–5). We cluster appointments geographically whenever possible to keep travel costs as low as possible for participants.

Can I refer for multiple services at the same time? +

Yes. Many of our participants receive multi-disciplinary care (e.g. Occupational Therapy and Speech Pathology, or Physiotherapy and Behaviour Support). On our online referral form, simply check all the services you are interested in, and our clinical intake coordinator will coordinate a cohesive service agreement.

What is a Functional Capacity Assessment (FCA) and why is it important? +

A Functional Capacity Assessment is a comprehensive clinical evaluation conducted by an Occupational Therapist. It evaluates how your disability impacts your ability to perform daily living tasks, self-care, social participation, and community access. It is considered the gold-standard evidence for NDIS plan reviews to justify funding for support workers, therapy budgets, and assistive technology.

How long does it take for a Behaviour Support Plan (BSP) to be lodged? +

If regulated restrictive practices are identified, our practitioner develops and lodges an Interim Behaviour Support Plan with the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission within 1 month. A Comprehensive Behaviour Support Plan, based on a full Functional Behaviour Assessment (FBA), is completed and lodged within 6 months.
