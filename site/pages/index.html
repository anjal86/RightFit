COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about working with RightFit Allied Health Services.

What funding types does RightFit accept? +

We accept NDIS participants who are Plan-Managed or Self-Managed across Capacity Building (e.g. Improved Daily Living, Improved Relationships) and Core budgets. We also welcome private-paying clients, Medicare Chronic Disease Management (CDM/EPC) plans from your GP, and private health insurance extras.

Do I need a doctor's referral to see an allied health therapist? +

No doctor's referral is required for private or NDIS-funded sessions. You or your Support Coordinator can submit a referral directly. A GP referral is only required if you plan to claim Medicare rebates under a Chronic Disease Management (CDM) plan.

How quickly can you see a new participant? +

We prioritise responsive intake. In most cases, our clinical intake coordinator reviews referrals within 1–2 business days to confirm capacity, discuss goals, and schedule an initial consultation without lengthy wait times.

Can your clinicians come to our home or school? +

Yes. A core part of our philosophy is providing community and mobile therapy where you are most comfortable — at home, school, day programmes, or workplaces. Telehealth sessions are also available Australia-wide.
